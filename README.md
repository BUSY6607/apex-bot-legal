# Apex Legal

Official legal documentation for **Apex**, an advanced Discord
moderation and community management bot.

This repository contains the official legal documents governing the use
of Apex, including its **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📄 Documents

### Privacy Policy

Explains:

-   What data Apex collects
-   Why the data is collected
-   How the data is stored
-   Data retention practices
-   Third-party services used
-   User privacy rights

**File:** - `privacy-policy.md`

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Terms of Service

Defines:

-   Eligibility requirements
-   Acceptable and prohibited use
-   Server administrator responsibilities
-   Service availability
-   Limitation of liability
-   Termination policy
-   Contact information

**File:** - `terms-of-service.md`

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

These documents are intended to provide transparency regarding how Apex
operates and how user and server data is handled.

The policies apply to every public deployment of Apex unless otherwise
stated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Third-Party Services

Apex currently relies on the following services:

-   Discord API
-   MongoDB Atlas

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.
